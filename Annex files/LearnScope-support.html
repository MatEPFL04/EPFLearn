Frequently asked

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-in and no account. Your quiz history and progress live only on your device, tied to this one install of the app.

Why did my progress disappear?

Because everything is stored locally rather than in an account, deleting the app, or offloading it without keeping its data, clears your history for good. There's currently no cloud backup to restore from.

If that's caused you real trouble, tell us: it's useful to know how much this matters to people.

I think I found a bug in a visualization

Send us: which topic it was in, what you were doing right before (e.g. "dragging the tangent point past x = 2"), and your iOS version. A screenshot or screen recording helps more than a paragraph of description.

Is LearnScope available on iPad?

Not yet: it's built for iPhone only right now. An iPad layout may follow later.

Can you add a topic that's missing?

We'd genuinely like to hear it. Name the subject and, if you can, the specific concept that felt underserved: that's more useful to us than "more content."
