LearnScope

Privacy Policy

Effective August 2, 2026

The short version: LearnScope collects nothing. There's no account, no analytics, no ads, and no network connection at all: your quiz progress lives only on your device.

What's stored on your device

A random local identifier. When you first open the app, it generates a random ID and saves it on your device. It isn't tied to your name, email, Apple ID, or any other personal information: it exists purely so the app can tell your quiz history apart from a fresh install.

Your quiz results. Which questions you've answered, whether you got them right, and when, stored locally alongside that identifier, so your progress page can show your history.

What we don't do

NO

Accounts or sign-in of any kind

NO

Network requests: the app never talks to a server

NO

Analytics, crash reporting, or usage tracking

NO

Advertising or ad identifiers

NO

Sharing data with third parties: there's nothing to share

NO

Selling data, in any form

Your data, your device

Because everything stays local, deleting the app deletes everything it stored: the identifier and your quiz history both. There's no server copy to separately request the removal of, since one never existed.

Children's privacy

LearnScope is built for first-year university engineering students and isn't directed at children. It doesn't knowingly collect any information from anyone, of any age: the "collects nothing" policy above applies uniformly, regardless of who's using it.

Changes to this policy

If what the app collects ever changes, this page will be updated and the "Effective" date at the top will change with it. Given the app works entirely offline by design, we don't expect that to happen often.
